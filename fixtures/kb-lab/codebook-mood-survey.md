# Codebook excerpt: daily mood survey (synthetic)

## Variable mood_score
mood_score is the participant's self-rated mood from 1 (very low) to 7 (very high).
Missing responses are coded as -99, never as zero.

## Variable sleep_hours
sleep_hours is the self-reported hours of sleep the night before, rounded to the nearest half hour.
Values above 16 are treated as entry errors and set to missing.
