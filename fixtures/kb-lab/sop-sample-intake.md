# Sample intake SOP (synthetic)

## Receiving samples
Samples arrive at the loading dock on weekdays before noon.
Check each tube label against the shipping manifest and log the arrival in the intake sheet.

## Storage
Intake samples are kept in the minus 80 freezer in room 214 for at most 24 months.
Every freezer box is labelled with the study code and the intake date.

## Discarding samples
Expired samples go to the biohazard bin after the lab manager signs the discard log.
