DUA: phs000123.v2.p1 (dbGaP, Sleep Cohort Data Use Agreement 2026-014)

## Consent groups
The sleep cohort's dbGaP release phs000123.v2.p1 has two consent groups: GRU (general research use) and HMB (health, medical and biomedical research). Analyses of the HMB group may not include non-health outcomes.

## Approved users
Only lab members named on the data use certification may open the release. Each new analyst is added through the institution's signing official before their first download.

## Where the data lives
The release stays on the lab's encrypted analysis server. Copies on laptops, cloud drives or hosted AI tools are outside the agreement.
