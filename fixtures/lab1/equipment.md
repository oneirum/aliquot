# Equipment SOP

Synthetic document for the lab1 trust harness. Not a real lab's procedure.

## Booking the centrifuge
Book the centrifuge in the shared Outlook calendar at least 42 hours ahead. A session on the centrifuge is capped at 180 minutes, and the centrifuge lives in room 329.

## Cleaning the centrifuge
Wipe the centrifuge with distilled water after every use and initial the green cleaning sheet taped beside it.

## Centrifuge fault
If the centrifuge shows an error code, switch it off, hang a white out-of-service tag on it and call the equipment desk at extension 4017.
