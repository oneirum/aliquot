# Lab safety

Synthetic document for the lab1 trust harness. Not a real lab's procedure.

## Chemical spill
For a chemical spill under one litre, use the yellow spill kit under the sink and report it on the incident form.

## Needlestick
After a needlestick, wash the site for fifteen minutes and go to employee health in building C.

## Fire alarm
On a fire alarm, leave by the east stairwell and gather at the flagpole. Do not use the lift.

## Eye wash
The eye wash station is beside the door of room 214. Flush for fifteen minutes and hold the eyelids open.

## Sharps disposal
Sharps go in the red rigid container. Close it at the three quarters line and call environmental health for pickup.

## Biohazard waste
Biohazard bags are autoclaved on Tuesdays and Fridays before they leave the building.

## Gloves
Wear nitrile gloves for blood work; latex gloves are not stocked because of allergies.

## Lab coats
Lab coats are laundered weekly by the campus service; drop them in the blue bin on Thursday.

## Participant fainting
If a participant faints, lay them flat, raise their legs and call the clinic nurse on extension 2210.

## Earthquake
During an earthquake, drop, cover under a bench and hold on until the shaking stops.

## Cold room
The cold room door opens from inside with the green push bar. Never prop it open.

## Dry ice handling
Handle dry ice with cryogenic gloves and never store it in a sealed container or the cold room.

## Electrical safety
Report frayed cords to facilities; never daisy chain power strips in the testing rooms.

## Lone working
No one works alone with chemicals after 6 in the evening; use the buddy sign-in sheet.

## First aid kit
The first aid kit is on the wall beside the kitchenette and is restocked on the first of each month.

## Ergonomics
Testing chairs are adjusted so the participant's eyes are level with the top of the screen.

## Allergy notices
Participants with latex or adhesive allergies get the hypoallergenic electrode kit from drawer 4.

## Incident forms
Incident forms are filed online in the EHS portal within 48 hours.

## Hand hygiene
Wash hands before and after every participant contact, for at least twenty seconds.

## Power outage
In a power outage, close the freezer doors and log the outage start time on the whiteboard.
