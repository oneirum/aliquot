# Onboarding for research assistants

Synthetic document for the lab1 trust harness. Not a real lab's procedure.

## Keycards
Request a keycard from the department front office in room 101 with your offer letter. Cards are activated within two business days.

## Timesheets
Submit timesheets in the payroll portal by noon every second Friday. Late timesheets are paid in the next cycle.

## Lab meeting
Lab meeting is on Wednesdays at 10 in the seminar room on the third floor. Each member presents once per semester.

## Human subjects training
Complete the CITI human subjects course, social and behavioral track, before your first participant contact.

## Data storage
Store study files only on the encrypted research drive mounted as R:. Never keep study files on a personal laptop.

## VPN access
Off campus, connect through the GlobalProtect VPN with the research profile before opening the research drive.

## Printing
The lab printer is named hazel-mfp. Print participant materials only in black and white on the second tray.

## Ordering supplies
Order consumables through the Marketplace punchout with the lab's budget string ending 4471. Orders over 500 dollars need the PI's approval.

## Email lists
Join the lab1-all list for announcements and the lab1-ra list for scheduling. The PI adds you after your first week.

## Code repository
Analysis code lives in the lab's GitLab group under projects named by study acronym. Never commit data files.

## Participant payments
Participants receive a 40 dollar gift card per completed visit, logged in the payment binder with the card's last four digits.

## Sick days
If you are sick on a session day, text the session lead before 7 in the morning so the participant can be rescheduled.

## Office hours
The PI holds office hours on Thursdays from 2 to 4 in room 3120. Students book a slot on the door sheet.

## Badge photos
Badge photos are taken at the campus card office on Mondays and Tuesdays only.

## Desk assignment
New assistants share the hot desks in room 3104. Personal items go in the grey lockers, not on the desks.

## Slack workspace
The lab's chat workspace is lab1-sleep. Use the #sessions channel for same-day schedule changes.

## Consent forms
Signed consent forms go in the locked cabinet in room 3110 the same day. Scan nothing until the coordinator asks.

## Session scripts
Read the session script word for word from the laminated card. Do not paraphrase task instructions.

## Weekend sessions
Weekend sessions run only with two trained assistants present. The building door code changes every month.

## Mentoring
Each new assistant is paired with a senior graduate student mentor for the first semester.

## Authorship
Assistants who contribute to data collection are acknowledged; authorship follows the lab's CRediT policy.

## Conference posters
Poster printing is covered once per year through the department's print shop with a two week lead time.

## Recruitment flyers
Recruitment flyers must carry the IRB stamp and may be posted only on approved boards in the clinic lobby.

## Phone screens
Phone screens follow the eligibility script and take about fifteen minutes. Record the outcome in the screening log.

## Adverse events
Report any adverse event to the coordinator within 24 hours, even if it seems minor.

## Key returns
Return keys and the keycard to the front office on your last day; the deposit comes back with your final pay.

## Lab notebook
Keep a dated electronic notebook in the lab's OneNote; one page per session, no loose paper.

## Quiet room
Room 3125 is the quiet room for cognitive testing. Phones stay outside in the basket by the door.

## Coffee fund
The coffee fund is 5 dollars a month, paid to the lab manager in the first week.

## Holiday closures
The lab closes between the winter holidays and New Year; no sessions are booked in that week.
