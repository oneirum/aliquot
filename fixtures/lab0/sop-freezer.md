# Freezer and sample intake SOP

Synthetic document for the lab0 first-use check. Not a real lab's procedure.

## Receiving samples
Samples arrive at the loading dock between 9 and 11 on weekdays. Sign the courier manifest and log the box number in the intake binder.

## Labelling tubes
Every cryovial gets a printed barcode label before it is frozen. Handwritten labels are not accepted.

## Freezer storage
Aliquots are stored in the minus eighty freezer in room 214, rack C. Samples are kept for seven years after study close.

## Thawing samples
Thaw an aliquot on wet ice for thirty minutes. Never refreeze a thawed aliquot.

## Freezer alarm
If the freezer alarm sounds, call facilities at extension 4410 and move the racks to the backup freezer in room 216.

## Sample disposal
Expired samples go into the red biohazard bin. Record the disposal date in the intake binder.

## Shipping samples
Outbound shipments use dry ice and leave on Mondays only, so that no box sits in transit over a weekend.
