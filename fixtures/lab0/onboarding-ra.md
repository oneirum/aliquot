# Research assistant onboarding note

Synthetic document for the lab0 first-use check. Not a real lab's onboarding.

## First week training
New research assistants complete human subjects training and the lab's data handling module in the first week.

## Building access
Request a keycard from the department office on your first day. It takes two business days to activate.

## Lab meetings
Lab meeting is on Thursdays at 2pm in room 301. Research assistants present once per semester.

## Timesheets
Submit your hours in the payroll portal every other Friday by noon.

## Computer accounts
The lab manager creates your server account. Restricted data is opened only on the lab workstation, never on a laptop.

## Equipment questions
Questions about the actigraphy watches go to the lab manager.
